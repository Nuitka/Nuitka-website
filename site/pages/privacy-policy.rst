################
 Privacy Policy
################

This privacy policy applies to the commercial offering of Nuitka,
including Nuitka commercial features, license management, and the Nuitka
subscription service.

*********************
 Current Data Policy
*********************

As of today, **Nuitka commercial does not collect, store, or process any
personal data** from its users. The commercial plugins and the Nuitka
compiler itself operate entirely on your local machine and do not
transmit any information back to us.

********************
 Future Data Policy
********************

In future versions, the Nuitka commercial software will perform the
following data processing to enable license validation and provide
update notifications:

**Abbreviated IP addresses**
   During license status checks and update queries, we will transmit and
   temporarily log **abbreviated IP addresses** (the last octet removed
   for IPv4, or the last 64 bits removed for IPv6). These truncated
   addresses do not allow identification of individual users or devices.

**Machine information**
   A basic **machine identifier** (derived from your system hardware or
   OS installation) will be transmitted to verify that a license is used
   within its permitted scope and to correlate update check requests.

No other information — such as your source code, compiled binaries, file
names, project configurations, or any personal data — will ever be
transmitted automatically.

.. note::

   These changes have not been implemented yet. This document will be
   updated with the exact details once the feature is released.

***********************
 Purpose and Retention
***********************

The data described above is collected exclusively for the following
purposes:

-  **License validation** — ensuring that a Nuitka commercial license is
   valid and used within its permitted terms.

-  **Update notifications** — informing users when a newer version of
   Nuitka commercial is available.

Abbreviated IP addresses and machine identifiers are stored only for the
duration of these operations and are automatically purged from our logs
within a reasonable timeframe (no longer than 90 days).

********************
 Legal Basis (GDPR)
********************

For users in the European Economic Area, the legal basis for the planned
processing is **Article 6(1)(b) GDPR** — processing necessary for the
performance of a contract (the commercial license agreement between you
and Nuitka Services).

**********************
 Third-Party Services
**********************

Nuitka commercial does not share data with any third-party analytics,
advertising, or tracking services. License validation and update checks
communicate exclusively with servers operated by or on behalf of Nuitka
Services.

*************
 Your Rights
*************

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

-  Request information about data we store about you.

-  Request correction or deletion of your data.

-  Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
