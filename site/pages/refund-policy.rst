###############
 Refund Policy
###############

Nuitka commercial subscriptions grant **access** to commercial features,
support information, and private Nuitka commercial packages. They do not
sell software licenses for a standalone product.

************
 No Refunds
************

**Refunds are not provided** for Nuitka commercial subscriptions unless
in very specific circumstances. Once access has been granted, the
subscription is considered fulfilled.

The standard (free) version of Nuitka can be used to verify that Nuitka
works for your project and meets your needs before purchasing a
commercial subscription.

******************
 Issue Resolution
******************

If you encounter problems using Nuitka commercial features:

-  **Higher-tier commercial users** (those include support packages) are
   entitled to issue resolution with priority support. Bugs affecting
   your use of commercial features will be fixed if possible, but we
   cannot give guarantees or time lines. But Sponsor level subscriptions
   are getting their stuff to be worked on first and immediately within
   48h.

-  For all other commercial users, the reports are handled via the
   private channels, but may not have priority.

Failure of Nuitka to compile or run your specific code, or the standard
version not working for your use case, is not grounds for a refund.
